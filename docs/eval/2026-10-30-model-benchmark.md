# Model benchmark — 2026-10-30

1876 labelled photos (iNaturalist via GBIF + Pl@ntNet), whole photo, prompt "a photo of {species}.". Cells: top-1 / top-3 (number of measurements). **Unseen** = uploaded on/after 2024-06-01: after the cut-off of BioCLIP (2023-11) and BioCLIP 2 (GBIF snapshot 2024-05-01). Cut-offs of BioCLIP 2.5 and BioTrove-CLIP are not published in the sources read, and generic models were trained on web data of unknown dates: their *unseen* column is probably, not provably, clean.
